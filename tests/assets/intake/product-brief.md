# Product brief

## Executive summary

A small club app where members book classes and coaches manage their rosters.

## Scope (MoSCoW)

- M1. Members can book a class.
- S1. Coaches can message a roster.
- W1. Payments are out of scope.

## Actors and permissions

| Actor  | Puede        | No puede                |
| ------ | ------------ | ----------------------- |
| Member | Book a class | See other members' data |

## Business rules

- RN-1. A member holds at most three active bookings.
